#Stock manager App

A flutter application to manage product stock quantities.

Features:-
- it has 3 product cards.
- can increase and decrese products quantities.
- prevent quantities from going below 5.
- it shows price of each unit price for each item.
- it has a grand total amount and it varies wehn each quantities increse.
- low stock warning when quantities goes below 5.


Concepts Used:-
- StatefullWidgets and StatelessWidgets used.
- reusable widgets.
- used constructors and Callbacks.
- setState().
- also used ListTile.
